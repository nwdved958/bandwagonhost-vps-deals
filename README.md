- **The 30-day money-back guarantee is real.** New orders can be refunded in full within 30 days under the official refund policy, so a wrong plan choice in the first month isn't money lost.

- **Uptime guarantees vary by family**: 99.9% on the base service, 99.95% on KVM PROMO plans, and a contractual 99.99% SLA on the E-Commerce line.
